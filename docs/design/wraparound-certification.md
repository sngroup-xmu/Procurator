# Wraparound Certification

Wraparound evidence must distinguish `ENTRY_CHECK`, `NEAR_WRAP`, and
`CLOSURE_CHECK`. Focused near-wrap artifacts are diagnostics or acceleration
evidence; they are not certified confirm or closure evidence by themselves.

Any inconclusive stage remains inconclusive. `TIMEOUT`, `UNKNOWN`, OOM,
toolchain `ERROR`, missing witnesses, and unverified `SAFE` results must not be
reported as bug absence.
